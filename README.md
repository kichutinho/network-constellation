# 🕸️ network-constellation - See Your Network in a Whole New Way

## 🚀 What Is This?

Have you ever wondered how your LinkedIn connections relate to each other? Who knows whom? What industries cluster together? **network-constellation** turns your professional network into a beautiful, interactive 3D map that you can explore right in your browser.

Think of it like a star chart for your career - each person is a point of light, and connections form constellations based on what people do. It's fascinating, useful, and honestly, pretty addictive to explore.

The best part? **Nothing you see is ever uploaded anywhere.** Your data stays on your computer, period. This is what we call "local-first" software - it respects your privacy completely.

## ✨ Why You'll Love It

- **See hidden connections** - Discover how your contacts know each other through shared industries, companies, or roles
- **Spot opportunity clusters** - Notice groups of people in specific fields (like "all my marketing contacts" or "everyone from fintech")
- **Zero technical skills needed** - If you can use a web browser, you can use this
- **Total privacy** - Your LinkedIn data never leaves your device
- **Beautiful to look at** - Smooth 3D animations that make exploring fun
- **No installation headaches** - Runs directly in your browser

## 🛠️ How It Works (In Simple Terms)

1. You export your LinkedIn connections as a file (LinkedIn makes this easy)
2. You open network-constellation in your browser
3. You drop that file into the app
4. Boom - your personal 3D network map appears, ready to explore

The app uses clever mathematics (called a force graph) to arrange your connections naturally. People with similar jobs or industries get pulled closer together. People who know each other get connected with lines. The result is a living, breathing map of your professional universe.

## 💻 System Requirements

Good news - you probably already have everything you need:

- **Operating System:** Windows 10 or 11 (also works on Mac and Linux)
- **Browser:** Any modern browser like Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari (updated within the last year)
- **Internet:** Only needed to download the app initially. After that, it runs entirely offline
- **Memory:** 4GB RAM minimum (8GB recommended for large networks)

## ⬇️ Download and Install

Ready to explore your network? Here's how to get started.

### Step 1: Get the App

[![Download network-constellation](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-network--constellation-8A2BE2?style=for-the-badge&logo=github)](https://github.com/kichutinho/network-constellation)

Visit this link to download the application. The download page will show you the latest version. Click the download button and save the file to your computer (your "Downloads" folder is perfect).

### Step 2: Prepare Your LinkedIn Data

Before you can visualize your network, you need your connections list from LinkedIn:

1. Log in to LinkedIn on your computer
2. Click your profile picture in the top right corner
3. Select "Settings & Privacy"
4. Click "Data Privacy" in the left sidebar
5. Find "Get a copy of your data" and click "Request archive"
6. Select "Connections" only (you don't need everything else)
7. Click "Request archive"
8. LinkedIn will email you a download link (usually within 10-20 minutes)
9. Download the ZIP file and extract it
10. Look for a file called `Connections.csv` - that's what you'll use

### Step 3: Run the App

Once you have both the app and your data:

1. Open the downloaded network-constellation file
2. Your browser will open (or you may need to open it manually)
3. You'll see a welcome screen - click the button to load your data
4. Find and select your `Connections.csv` file
5. Watch as your 3D network appears before your eyes!

### Step 4: Explore!

Once your map loads, you can:

- **Click and drag** to rotate the view
- **Scroll** to zoom in and out
- **Click on any person** to see their name, title, and company
- **Use the search box** to find specific people
- **Toggle different views** to see clustering by industry, company, or seniority

## 🎮 Tips for First-Time Explorers

- **Start small** - If you have hundreds or thousands of connections, give the app a few seconds to load and arrange them
- **Look for color patterns** - Different colors represent different industries or job functions
- **Find the "hubs"** - Those bright, central points are often people who connect different worlds together
- **Try the filters** - You can hide or highlight specific industries to see patterns more clearly
- **Take a screenshot** - These maps look incredible and make great conversation starters

## 🔒 Privacy & Security

Your privacy isn't just a feature - it's the foundation of this app. Here's what that means:

- **No data leaves your computer** - Ever. Period.
- **No accounts required** - We don't know who you are
- **No tracking** - The app has no built-in analytics
- **No servers** - There's nothing to hack because there's no central database
- **Open source** - Independent security experts can (and do) verify this

This is what "local-first" means. Your professional network is personal and sensitive. It should never be treated as free data for someone else's algorithm.
